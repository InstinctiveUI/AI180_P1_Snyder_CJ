# Position Statement

**Project:** 3D Model Lifecycle Pipeline
**Date:** 2026-05-11

---

## My Stance

I am pursuing the creative pipeline of creating models in video game development and a way to help people in my field bypass a breakneck in the creation process

## What Matters Most

Making sure that this program and in extension AI is a thinking partner and not just a plug and pray tool, this tool should enable people to get work done faster but not do all the work and thinking for you.

The pipeline of creation and the files themselves must survive, as well as the file formats and types. The decision-making itself should stay on the human side — making sure that the person behind the screen is the one thinking and deciding everything about the file at all times.

## What I Will Not Compromise On

The AI will not cross the line by making its own decisions. Everything that the AI does has to be decided by me and something that I ask for and give reason for.

I will not compromise on the use case of what I create, what type of program I am using, and what it is doing to the files with it. It is a specific use case — that's it.
