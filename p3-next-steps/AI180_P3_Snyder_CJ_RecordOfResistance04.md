# Record of Resistance

**Course:** AI 180
**Project:** P3 — 3D Model Lifecycle Pipeline
**Date:** 2026-05-18
**Record #:** 04

---

## What AI Suggested

When building the P3 systems map, Claude proposed three leverage points based on its own reading of the pipeline: export settings in 3ds Max/ZBrush, the transfer tool, and engine import settings in Unity/Unreal. The framing treated all three as roughly equal.

> "Based on what you described, here are three candidates — tell me if these match your experience or correct them."

---

## Why I Rejected or Revised It

The three options were presented as a balanced set, but that framing flattened what I actually know about the pipeline. From working in it, the transfer tool is not just one leverage point among three — it is the central one. It is the moment where the model moves from one context to another, and where the most consequential decisions about format, scale, and mesh integrity happen. Treating it as equal to the others would have made the map less accurate, not more.

---

## What I Did Instead

I redirected the leverage point framing to put the transfer tool at the center — the handoff point between creation software and game engine. The other two leverage points remained, but the map now reflects their relationship to the transfer tool rather than presenting them as independent variables.
