# Research: Game Development Lifecycle Stages

**Source:** "Stages of the Game Development Process" — Game Design Skills
**URL:** https://gamedesignskills.com/game-development/stages-of-game-development-process/

**What was consulted:** An article outlining the full stages of game development, from pre-production through release.

**When:** During P3, while building the systems map.

**What I took from it:** The breakdown of development stages helped identify the actors and phases in the systems map — specifically, where 3D model creation sits within the larger pipeline, and how the handoff between authoring tools and game engines fits into the broader lifecycle. The article grounded the map in an established framework rather than just my own experience.
