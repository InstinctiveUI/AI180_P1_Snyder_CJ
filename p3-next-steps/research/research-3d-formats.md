# Research: 3D File Format Transfer Issues

**Source type:** Community forums — Reddit

**What was consulted:** Reddit threads discussing common problems when transferring 3D models between applications — axis mismatches, scale inconsistencies, non-manifold geometry, inverted normals, and format-specific quirks.

**When:** During P2, while building the initial transfer tool and knowledge base.

**What I took from it:** The common failure modes that became the foundation of `knowledge_base.py` and the `3D_Model_Transfer_Issues.xlsx` spreadsheet in the repo root. Reddit threads from practitioners in game development and 3D modeling provided real-world examples of what breaks and why — not just documentation-level descriptions.

**Note:** No specific threads were bookmarked or preserved. This was lived research — pattern recognition across multiple posts over time, not a single cited source.
