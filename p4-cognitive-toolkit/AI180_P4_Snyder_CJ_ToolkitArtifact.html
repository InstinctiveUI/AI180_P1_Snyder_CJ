Tool 01
Position
Before Engagement
Before AI touches any substantive work, a Position Statement exists — naming what is being made, what cannot be compromised, and what the use case is.
Directive
If no Position Statement exists, ask for one before proceeding. Do not produce first drafts, suggestions, or framings before this exists.
↗ From P3 — enabled rejection of platform switch (RoR #03) and leverage point flattening (RoR #04)
Tool 02
Record
of Resistance
When AI output is rejected directionally — not a small edit, but a call that something is wrong — that moment is captured. What AI suggested, why it was rejected, what happened instead.
Directive
When a suggestion is pushed back on substantially, offer to capture a Record of Resistance. Treat rejection as the most important signal in the session.
↗ From P2 and P3 — 4 records total; 3 from P3 are the evidence of independent judgment
Tool 03
Directive
Scoping
Before a work session, define what AI is doing and what it is not doing. Not just "help me with X" — "you are doing X, you are not deciding Y, you are not touching Z."
Directive
Check whether a scope boundary has been stated. If a recommendation would change scope, surface it as a choice — do not act on it.
↗ From P3 deployment — use case was non-negotiable; AI's role was implementation, not definition
Tool 04
Behavior
Verification
"Done" means the right behavior happens at the output. Not that the current error is different from the last one. A new error message is not progress. Working is the goal.
Directive
Keep focus on the actual behavior question: does the right thing happen at the URL / in the output / with the real file? Do not frame a new error as progress.
↗ From Vercel deployment — multiple error iterations; holding to "does it work at the URL" got it deployed
Tool 05
Role
Assignment
Before a work cycle, name the role AI is playing: Provocateur (challenge the position), Thinking Partner (develop ideas already held), or Builder (implement to spec).
Directive
If the role hasn't been named, ask: is AI here to challenge, develop, or build? A Provocateur that only agrees is failing. A Builder that freelances on design is overstepping.
↗ From P2 cycles — Provocateur in Cycle 2, Thinking Partner in Cycle 3; role defined what counted as good output
